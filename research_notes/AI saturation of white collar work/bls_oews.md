# BLS OEWS National Estimates for U.S. White-Collar Occupations (May 2025)

Note on scope and method: All figures come from the official BLS OEWS **May 2025** national data file (`national_M2025_dl.xlsx` inside [oesm25nat.zip](https://www.bls.gov/oes/special-requests/oesm25nat.zip)). That release is published: BLS regional OEWS releases dated June–August 2026 use it, and its technical notes were last modified May 15, 2026. The May 2024 file ([oesm24nat.zip](https://www.bls.gov/oes/special-requests/oesm24nat.zip)) was also pulled for comparison. The HTML table pages on bls.gov (for example https://www.bls.gov/oes/current/oes_nat.htm) returned **403 "Access Denied" (bot block)**, but the official zip downloads worked, so **no mirrors or third-party sites were used** for any number. The release does not give national figures for "Emp" and "Mean wage" combined rows, so where a row sums several SOC codes, the components are BLS figures, the employment sum is simple addition, and the "emp-weighted mean wage" is **my own derived figure** (sum of emp × mean ÷ total emp, using BLS's rounded means). It is not an official BLS statistic.

## Exact employment and mean annual wage for each listed occupation (latest OEWS release)

### Takeaway
The most recent release is **OEWS May 2025**. Its reference period is May 2025, and it pools six semiannual panels from November 2022 to May 2025. It reports **155,495,730** total U.S. wage-and-salary jobs with an all-occupation mean annual wage of **$69,770**. Every requested SOC code was found in the file, and no requested value is suppressed or top-coded.

### Cited Findings

**Release and methods**
- The May 2025 estimates use six semiannual panels: May 2025, Nov 2024, May 2024, Nov 2023, May 2023, Nov 2022. Each establishment's population employment is the average of its May 2025 and November 2024 employment, taken from QCEW. — [BLS OEWS May 2025 Technical Notes](https://www.bls.gov/oes/2025/may/oes_tec.htm)
- OEWS excludes "the self-employed, owners and partners in unincorporated firms, household workers, or unpaid family workers." Private households (NAICS 814) and most of agriculture are also excluded. This matters for occupations with heavy self-employment, such as real estate agents, lawyers, writers, graphic designers, interpreters, financial advisors and insurance agents. — [BLS OEWS May 2025 Technical Notes](https://www.bls.gov/oes/2025/may/oes_tec.htm)
- The October 1–November 12, 2025 federal funding lapse "required additional collection and processing time" for the May 2025 panel. BLS said the response rate was within the normal range and that no methodology changes were needed. — [BLS OEWS May 2025 Technical Notes](https://www.bls.gov/oes/2025/may/oes_tec.htm)
- Employment is rounded to the nearest 10 and wages to the nearest $10 in the BLS file. Source for every number in the tables below: [BLS OEWS May 2025 national data file, oesm25nat.zip](https://www.bls.gov/oes/special-requests/oesm25nat.zip). The May 2024 columns come from [oesm24nat.zip](https://www.bls.gov/oes/special-requests/oesm24nat.zip).

### Inferences
- The requested occupations cover about 56.0M jobs at the detailed level, against 87.1M in the 12 major groups. Groups 29 (Healthcare), 41 (Sales) and 43 (Office) contribute most of the gap (see the next section).
- Notable changes from May 2024 to May 2025: Medical Secretaries rose 830,760 → 961,610 (+16%). Customer Service Reps fell 2.73M → 2.60M, Bookkeeping Clerks 1.46M → 1.37M, and Computer Programmers 109,870 → 92,230 (−16%). Writers & Editors fell 198,810 → 185,130 and Graphic Designers 214,260 → 197,830. Data Scientists rose 233,440 → 262,440 (+12%). Total Office & Admin Support (43) fell by about 465k while total employment rose by about 1.3M. Year-to-year OEWS comparisons should be treated with caution, because each estimate pools three years of panels and BLS does not design OEWS as a time series.
- Because OEWS excludes the self-employed, its figures for Real Estate Agents (193k), Lawyers, Writers, Graphic Designers, Interpreters and Personal Financial Advisors are well below total-workforce counts such as those in the CPS. If the data file counts all workers, keep this definitional difference in mind.

### Gaps
- The bls.gov HTML pages (oes_nat.htm and per-occupation oesXXXXXX.htm) were blocked with a 403 bot block. The official BLS zip file was the sole data source, so no numbers needed a mirror.
- The exact national release date of May 2025 OEWS was not confirmed. Regional May 2025 releases are dated June–August 2026 ([example](https://www.bls.gov/regions/west/news-release/2026/occupationalemploymentandwages_lewiston_20260626.htm)), and the technical notes were last modified May 15, 2026.
- BLS publishes no mean wages for combined rows, so the "~ derived" values are my calculations from rounded BLS means. Treat them as approximate to within about ±$10.
